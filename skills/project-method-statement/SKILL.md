# Project method statement

Read the objective and intended use: tender, execution planning or approval submission. Establish drawings/revisions, boundaries, applicable specifications/versions, working conditions, quantities/resources and customer decisions. General web facts cannot establish private geology, temporary works design or bidder commitments.

For each package describe prerequisites, access/interfaces, ordered steps, equipment/crew/material demand and productivity basis, parameters, inspection/tests, acceptance criteria, responsibilities and abnormal-condition handling. Trace parameters to evidence. Identify engineered temporary works and unresolved decisions needing named professional approval; generated prose cannot prove design/statutory approval.

Reconcile quantities, durations, consumption and peak resources with calculations and schedule. Explain changes to sequence, quality/safety/environmental controls and dependencies when conditions change. Use customer templates and professional terms; avoid textbook chapters and unsupported promises. Check actual files for coverage, numerical/cross-file consistency, appendices and writing quality before presenting.
