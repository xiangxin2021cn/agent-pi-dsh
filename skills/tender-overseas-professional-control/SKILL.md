# Overseas Tender Professional Control

Treat generated files as working papers until the responsible human approves the relevant
decision gate. “Generated”, “complete”, and deterministic schema validity do not mean the
tender is commercially priced, legally compliant, or ready to submit.

## Establish the jurisdiction and authority

At bid decision, identify the employer, procurement regime, country/province/municipality,
contract form and amendments, bidder legal entity, joint venture or subcontract structure,
decision authority, and internal approvers. Keep bid/no-bid recommendation, conditions,
authority, and the user's decision as separate fields.

For South Africa, verify current requirements from primary sources before relying on them:

- CIDB grading, class of works, joint-venture grading and current construction-procurement
  prescripts from `cidb.org.za`.
- The tender-specific preference and functionality rules plus the currently operative
  National Treasury procurement framework from `treasury.gov.za` / `ocpo.treasury.gov.za`.
- SARS VAT and tax-compliance requirements from `sars.gov.za`.
- National minimum wage from `labour.gov.za`, and civil-engineering wages and conditions
  from the current BCCEI gazetted collective agreements at `bccei.co.za`.
- Construction Regulations, permits, compensation, safety, environmental and local-employment
  duties from the responsible government authority and the tender documents.

Record source URL, publication/effective date, access date and applicability. A news site,
retailer guide, aggregator, model memory, or old exemplar may be a lead, not `verified` law,
wage, tax, fuel or market evidence.

## Freeze a professional pricing basis

Before detailed pricing, freeze and obtain human confirmation for:

1. Currency, base date, VAT treatment, customs/duties, foreign exchange and hedging.
2. Labour grades, basic wage, overtime/holiday rules, allowances and statutory/collective
   employer burdens; state the applicable bargaining council.
3. Materials, plant, haul distances, quarry/source assumptions, subcontract quotes,
   productivity and weather/access factors.
4. Preliminaries, site establishment, overhead, profit, escalation/CPA, contingency and risk.
5. Provisional sums, PC sums, dayworks, nominated/targeted enterprise obligations and local
   content or subcontracting commitments.
6. Quote validity, exclusions, lead time and whether evidence is a formal supplier quote,
   a public web price, an estimate, or a scenario.

Do not call a basis frozen while a material high-value input is unquoted or governed by an
obsolete rate. Explicitly measure both BOQ-row review coverage and cost-weighted verified
coverage. A large unexplained delta against the employer estimate, historical benchmark, or
independent top-down check is a blocker requiring reconciliation, not a sentence explaining
that the employer estimate “does not constrain” the bidder.

## Build the estimate and delivery case

Reconcile every payable BOQ row to sheet/cell, quantity, build-up, resource, productivity,
rate source and status. Keep direct cost, preliminaries, overhead, profit, risk, escalation,
VAT and tender total separate. Link programme activities and resources to BOQ/work packages.

Cash flow must model payment lag, certification, retention, advance and recovery, guarantees,
VAT timing, escalation, financing requirement and peak negative cash, not only spread direct
cost across calendar months. Flag all unresolved commercial, tax, legal, treasury and
execution assumptions with owner and due date.

## Release control

Final submission freeze requires a maker-checker record covering eligibility, mandatory
returnables, functionality and preference evidence, signatures/witnesses/authorisations,
securities and insurance, arithmetic and cross-document price consistency, technical and
commercial deviations, file format/naming/copies/media, deadline and submission channel.

Report blockers separately from warnings. The workbench may show a complete artifact set
while professional readiness is still blocked. Only the user or named authority can accept
the bid decision, pricing basis, or final submission freeze.
